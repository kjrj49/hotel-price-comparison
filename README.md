## What “cheap hotels” should mean in practice

A cheap hotel is not necessarily the property with the lowest nightly rate. A more useful comparison looks at the full cost and the conditions attached to it.

Before choosing a room, compare:

- Total price after taxes and mandatory fees
- Cancellation deadline
- Whether payment is due now or at the property
- Whether breakfast, parking, or resort access is included
- Room size and bed configuration
- Guest rating and recent review volume
- Whether the booking is refundable
- Whether the rate is prepaid, pay-at-hotel, or partially refundable
- Whether the reservation is made directly with the hotel or through an online travel agency

For example, a room listed at $92 per night may become more expensive than a $105 room after taxes, resort fees, parking, and a non-refundable booking condition are included. The cheaper headline rate is only useful if it remains cheaper at the final payment screen.

ShopBack’s own hotel guidance recommends keeping the provider’s payable total and the potential cashback amount separate during comparison. Combining them too early can make different room conditions appear equivalent when they are not.

## Which booking site is most likely to have the cheapest hotel?

There is no permanent winner. Pricing changes according to destination, dates, inventory, currency, cancellation terms, and the relationship between the hotel and the booking provider.

A useful comparison usually looks like this:

### Booking.com for broad inventory

Booking.com is often useful when you want to compare hotels, apartments, guesthouses, and smaller independent properties in one search. Its inventory can be especially broad in international destinations.

The important limitation is that the displayed price is not the whole decision. Check taxes, fees, cancellation rules, payment timing, and whether the property is fulfilled through an external partner. ShopBack’s Booking.com guidance also lists common exclusions such as coupon use, some package and flight bookings, Booking.basic reservations, external partner pages, and taxes or service charges.

### Hotels.com for hotel-focused searches

Hotels.com is worth checking when you are booking standard hotels, resorts, or longer stays and can use One Key features. Member-only rates and OneKeyCash may affect the final value, but they should be compared against the actual checkout price rather than added together automatically.

A lower room price with no cancellation may still be a poor choice if your schedule is uncertain. A slightly higher refundable rate can be cheaper in practice if the trip changes and the non-refundable booking would otherwise be lost.

### Agoda for international and resort stays

Agoda can be competitive for hotels in Asia and other international destinations. Its AgodaVIP label identifies participating properties with special prices, but it does not mean every hotel or every room receives the same discount. The offer applies to the specific property and rate that displays the VIP badge.

Agoda’s ShopBack category is hotel-specific in the US listing. Flights, packages, activities, rental cars, and airport transfers may be excluded, so do not assume that cashback applies to every item in a mixed travel cart.

### Expedia and Priceline for package or discount comparisons

Expedia is useful when you are comparing hotel-only bookings with flight-and-hotel packages. A package can sometimes be cheaper overall, but its cancellation rules and cashback category may differ from a standalone hotel reservation.

Priceline can be useful for travelers who are comfortable with more restrictive rates or opaque hotel offers. Read the terms before paying. A room that reveals fewer details before purchase may offer a lower price, but that reduction comes with less control.

### Direct hotel bookings for loyalty benefits

For chain hotels, compare the third-party price with the hotel’s own website. A direct booking may include loyalty points, elite credit, easier changes, or better handling when something goes wrong. A third-party booking may show a lower headline price or cashback, but hotel loyalty benefits can be limited on some prepaid or agency reservations.

The cheapest option depends on what you value:

- Lowest immediate price: compare all-in totals across providers.
- Maximum flexibility: choose a refundable rate with a clear cancellation deadline.
- Hotel loyalty credit: check the direct hotel rate and program terms.
- Cashback: start the booking through the eligible ShopBack merchant page.
- Long stay: include cleaning fees, resort fees, service fees, and payment timing.

## How to find genuinely cheap hotels

### Move the dates before lowering the hotel standard

Changing the stay by one or two nights can reduce the price more than moving from a four-star hotel to a three-star hotel. Search flexible dates where possible, especially for city breaks, weekend stays, and event-heavy destinations.

ShopBack’s travel guidance recommends shifting dates by one to three days and using price alerts before committing to a booking.

### Compare the same room, not just the same hotel

A hotel may show several rooms with different terms:

- King room, non-refundable
- King room, free cancellation
- King room with breakfast
- King room with partial sea view
- King room with payment at the property

These are different products. Compare like with like before deciding that one site is cheaper.

### Check the final total

The final total should include:

- Base room rate
- Taxes
- Resort or destination fees
- Service fees
- Cleaning fees for vacation rentals
- Parking
- Breakfast
- Extra guest charges
- Currency conversion fees, if applicable

For vacation rentals, a low nightly price can be outweighed by cleaning and service fees. For resort destinations, mandatory property charges may not be obvious in the first search result.

### Use cashback after choosing the right room

Cashback should be the final layer of the comparison. First choose the hotel, room, dates, and terms. Then open the merchant through ShopBack and confirm the current offer.

This avoids a common mistake: choosing a more expensive room simply because it displays a higher cashback percentage. A 5% reward on a $180 room is not automatically better than a 2% reward on a $140 room, especially if the cheaper room has the same conditions.

### Avoid breaking the tracking session

For the best chance of receiving cashback:

1. Start from ShopBack.
2. Open the relevant hotel merchant.
3. Use one device and one browser.
4. Complete the booking without clicking another cashback or advertising link.
5. Avoid private browsing and aggressive ad-blocking tools during checkout.
6. Check the merchant terms before applying a coupon.
7. Keep your booking confirmation.

ShopBack’s Booking.com instructions specifically warn that switching browsers or devices, using privacy tools that block tracking, leaving the session, or applying excluded promo codes can prevent cashback from tracking.

## Can hotel discounts and cashback be combined?

Sometimes, but never assume it.

There are several different types of savings:

- Merchant sale price
- Member-only hotel price
- Promo code
- Loyalty currency
- Credit card rewards
- ShopBack cashback

Each follows its own terms. Some merchant pages allow certain promotions to stack with cashback. Others exclude bookings that use a coupon or external discount code.

Booking.com cashback commonly excludes bookings where a promo, coupon, or discount code is applied unless the ShopBack merchant terms explicitly say otherwise.

Agoda’s published guidance also warns that promotions generally cannot be combined with other promotions or loyalty programs unless the terms state otherwise. An AgodaVIP badge should therefore be treated as a price to compare, not proof that every other reward will apply on top.

The safest calculation is:

> Final checkout price minus confirmed cashback equals effective cost.

Do not subtract cashback that is only displayed as “up to,” and do not count a reward that has not yet been confirmed. Cashback can be rejected after cancellation, refund, no-show, or a booking change that creates a new reservation ID.

## Common mistakes that make a hotel booking more expensive

### Choosing by nightly price only

The nightly rate is often the first number displayed, not the final amount. Always compare the total payable amount.

### Ignoring cancellation rules

A non-refundable room can be cheaper, but only if your plans are settled. If the trip may move, a refundable room can have better value even with a higher initial price.

### Assuming the hotel will honor every loyalty benefit

Third-party bookings may not earn the same hotel points, elite credit, upgrades, or status benefits as direct bookings. Check the specific hotel program before deciding.

### Applying a random coupon after activating cashback

A coupon found elsewhere may void cashback. Read the current merchant terms before using it.

### Booking through the hotel’s mobile app after starting on ShopBack

If the cashback click opens a merchant page, complete the booking through that flow. Opening a separate app or restarting the search can break attribution.

### Comparing different payment schedules

A pay-now room and a pay-at-hotel room are not identical. One may use a different exchange rate, cancellation rule, or deposit requirement.

## A practical cheap-hotel checklist

Before paying, confirm the following:

- The hotel and address are correct.
- The room type matches what you need.
- The guest count is correct.
- The final total includes all mandatory charges.
- The cancellation deadline is clear.
- The payment date is understood.
- Breakfast and parking are either included or intentionally excluded.
- The booking provider is acceptable for your loyalty goals.
- The ShopBack merchant page shows the relevant hotel category.
- Cashback was activated before checkout.
- No excluded coupon or external promotion was applied.
- The confirmation email has been saved.

For a simple hotel search, the most reliable sequence is to compare Booking.com, Hotels.com, Agoda, Expedia, Priceline, and the hotel’s direct rate where relevant. Then return to the route offering the best combination of total price, flexibility, and booking conditions.
